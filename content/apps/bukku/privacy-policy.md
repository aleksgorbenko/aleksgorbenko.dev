Bukku does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data off your device.

- **No account.** Bukku requires no sign-in.
- **No cloud.** Your tabs, bookmarks, and history are read locally from the
  browsers installed on your Mac and never leave it.
- **No telemetry.** Bukku does not track usage or send analytics.
- **No third parties.** No data is shared with anyone.

Browser access is granted by you through macOS permissions and is used solely to
provide search and tab-management inside the app.

_Last updated: 2026-07-31._
