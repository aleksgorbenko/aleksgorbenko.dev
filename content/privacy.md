## Analytics

This site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) for aggregate
visitor stats (pageviews, referrers, rough location, browser). It doesn't use
cookies, and doesn't store your IP address, full User-Agent string, or any
other tracker ID. No data is shared with third parties.
